Hosting is live on Cloudflare. Domain purchase/connection, a professional mailbox, account-protection verification, automatic inquiry delivery, Turnstile, and search/analytics setup are separate stages; follow the handbook's current-status notes. The present consultation form prepares a draft that the visitor must send.
